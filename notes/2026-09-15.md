## Key concepts learned
- **Shell literal-ing:** quotes, backslash-escaping, `./` prefix, and Tab-completion all force a token to be read as a filename, not options/multiple args.
- **`find` by metadata:** locate files by size (`-size Nc`), permission (`! -executable`), owner/group (`-user`/`-group`), scope (`.` vs `/`). Know where you are (`pwd`) vs. what path you point at.
- **stdout vs stderr:** `2>/dev/null` discards permission-denied noise while keeping real results — essential when searching `/`.
- **Pipelines:** `|` chains single-purpose tools. `sort | uniq -u` (uniq needs sorted input), `strings | grep` to pull readable data out of binaries.
- **`file` vs name:** identify content by reading bytes, not by extension.
